A Bit About Me 👨🏻
Intro
I'm a protocol engineer passionate about blockchain infrastructure, AI systems, and full-stack development. My work sits at the intersection of decentralized systems, machine learning, and game theory — fields I explore both professionally and in my own time.

Over the past several years, I've focused on building production-grade decentralized infrastructure: designing protocol architectures from V1 through multiple iterations, authoring smart contracts for protocol Nodes that raised ~800 ETH, architecting tokenomics and launching mainnets for Powerloom and Onchain Points, building high-throughput gasless EIP-712 relayers, and shipping Onchain Points Systems that power auto-resolving prediction markets. I've also led incentivized testnets over a span of 2 years, and single-handedly built BDS (Blockchain Data Services), a production decentralized data market streaming real-time DeFi data with cryptographic IPFS proofs.

Outside of work, I'm active in the open source community, enjoy building tools to trade crypto assets. I'm always eager to discuss protocols, data markets, zero-knowledge systems, or AI agents — feel free to reach out at xadahiya@gmail.com.
